Four invisible thingsa walk through the project

Storyboard for the IAC 2026 talk. Each iPoster box is a room; each room moves scene by scene with a click, in step with the script. The dark case is a 16:9 slide (drawing, controls, caption and counter), exactly what the iframe shows. The serif text is what is said aloud.

Each room makes one invisible thing visible: the radiation, the shield, the dose and the error. The last one was the hardest.

Opening. The whole poster on screen, no box open. The floor plan is the poster: the four rooms are the four boxes of the 2×2, and the route follows the script. Click a room to go to it.

Exit

Closing. Back to the full poster. The four rooms read as one corridor: rain, shield, dose, error. End on “where we still have to apply it” and add nothing after “Thank you”.

The philosophy, unspoken. The first three rooms are physics; the fourth is method. The talk does not promise a number: it promises knowing when a number deserves trust. And the best shield is the one that leaves the crew’s room calm.

Full script

    How this goes into the iPoster

    One room, one iframe

    Each box loads a GitHub Pages page with its slide. Click the slide to advance, click its left third to go back, or use the arrow keys; each advance matches a [CLICK] mark in the script.

    Controls inside the scene

    Buttons and sliders live inside the slide, along its bottom edge, and every scene keeps the same 16:9 frame. The presenter uses them with the box expanded, and any visitor afterwards. No scene needs them: each one starts on its own in the state that tells the story.

    Fallback

    The last scene of each room is exported as an image for the iPoster, in case the iframe does not load or does not take clicks at the venue. Still to confirm in the editor: the expanded iframe accepts clicks and drags.

    The four slides on their own

    What each iframe loads: room I, room II, room III and room IV. Each fills the window without distortion; click to advance, left third to go back, or the arrow keys.
